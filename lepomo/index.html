Lepomo

The calmest way to focus.

Lepomo is a calm, native Pomodoro timer for Mac. It lives in your menu bar, has a desktop widget, shows quiet insights by day, week, month and year — and works without an account. Your history stays on your Mac.

Lepomo is free. Lepomo Plus adds custom durations, full history, and extra themes and sounds.
