Quick answers

The timer didn't notify me. Check System Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ Lepomo. The timer itself keeps working even with notifications off.

I bought Plus on another Mac. Open Settings ▸ Plus ▸ Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription? System Settings ▸ your name ▸ Media & Purchases ▸ Subscriptions, or the App Store ▸ Account.

Where is my data? On your Mac only. Deleting the app deletes it.
