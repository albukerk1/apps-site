Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Lepomo is a focus timer made by Matheus Albuquerque ("we"). It is designed to work without knowing who you are.

What stays on your device

Your timer state, focus history, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We have no server that receives them, and the app has no account or login.

What leaves your device

  • Purchases. If you buy Lepomo Plus, the purchase is processed by Apple. To confirm that a purchase is valid and to restore it on your other devices, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier, the purchase receipt from Apple and basic technical information (such as app version and the country of the store). We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Nothing else. Lepomo contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device to tell you when a session ends.

Your choices

You can delete all app data by deleting the app. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Lepomo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address. Questions: matheusalbuquerque94@gmail.com.
